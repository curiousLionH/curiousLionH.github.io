# Joohyun Lee

Research Engineer — Autonomous Driving · M.S. Computer Science, Georgia Tech

<a href="mailto:joohyun@gatech.edu">joohyun@gatech.edu</a> · <a href="https://github.com/curiousLionH">GitHub</a> · <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/joohyun-lee-2b3816125">LinkedIn</a>

I build perception systems for autonomous vehicles, focusing on 3D perception,
multi-sensor fusion, and the practical deployment of tracking and LiDAR pipelines.
At Hyundai Motor Company I have worked on calibration automation, perception-system
validation, CUDA-based LiDAR interfaces, and camera-based end-to-end multi-object
tracking. I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, with a
project on diffusion-based 3D point cloud registration.
